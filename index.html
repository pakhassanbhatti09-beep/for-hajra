Hajra 🌹

Some people arrive quietly… and somehow stay unforgettable.

You don’t feel like just a friend — you feel like a rare exception.

I still remember your first message: “Aap ki age kia hai taake main aap ko reels send kar sakun?”

From that moment, comfort felt effortless.

I never thought I’d feel this ease opening up beyond my own world.

It’s been nine months — and I’m truly grateful to Allah for your friendship.

You became warmth on ordinary days… and a thought I don’t rush away.

Since you kept tapping… I’ll admit this softly — you cross my mind more than I let on.

I don’t name this feeling. Not because it’s small — but because it deserves care.

If one day this ever means more… I hope you’ll know — it was never timepass.
